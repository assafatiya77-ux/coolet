# Coolet Music — Iconic Moments and I WAS THERE

Status: approved requirement, specification only; not yet implemented in Android.

## Experience
- Every venue and show card has an **Iconic Moments / רגעים אייקוניים** gallery for photos and short videos, with artist, venue, date and credit.
- **I WAS THERE / הייתי שם** lets fans take or select selfies, photos or videos from a concert and make a shareable Coolet card.
- Bands can post group photos, backstage moments, rehearsals and audience encounters; venue accounts can post their own authorized media.
- Browsing by city, venue, band and concert; editorially curated historic imagery must be distinguished from community uploads.
- User chooses private storage by default or submission for public display. Public content requires moderation and rights confirmation.
- Include reporting, takedown, removal, user deletion, privacy protection, attribution and copyright safeguards.
- Sharing a public card can link back to the event or venue, helping fans and bands organically promote Coolet.
- Design: original black-white-red punk zine aesthetic inspired by 1970s NYC and the *era* depicted in Please Kill Me, without copying copyrighted artwork; preserve the Coolet logo, city decks, venue cards and Joker.

## Delivery milestones
1. Android photo/video picker and local private I WAS THERE card prototype.
2. Show and venue association; locally browsable galleries.
3. Secure accounts, uploads, consent and moderation before any public gallery.
4. Share links, band/venue profiles, curation, end-to-end Android tests and APK build.

No upload backend, moderation system, public gallery, or verified live sync exists yet.
