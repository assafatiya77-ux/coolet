# Coolet Music — first-revenue pilot

Status: proposal; no paying customers, partner agreements or revenue yet.

1. Ship a functional Android MVP with NYC venue cards, sourced current event listings, ticket links, private I WAS THERE card creation and sharing.
2. Recruit 5-10 active NYC clubs and bands as free pilot partners; ask permission to display promotional material, confirm official calendars and test QR code links at venues.
3. Measure venue-card visits, verified outbound ticket clicks, cards created and voluntary shares, with consent and privacy safeguards.
4. Offer optional paid promotional placement to partners only after demonstrating usage. Pilot pricing hypothesis: $49-$99 per month per venue/band, not a market-validated rate; clearly label paid promotion.
5. Explore affiliate ticket/vinyl revenue only after written agreements and platform approval; never assume commissions are available.
6. First target: one paying partner after a working demo and real pilot results. No claim of guaranteed sales.
7. No purchases, subscriptions, ad spend or publication without user approval.

Technical next steps: integrate current licensed event data, build Android cards and media picker, CI build and phone tests. Existing HTML prototype is a local demonstration, not deployed app.
